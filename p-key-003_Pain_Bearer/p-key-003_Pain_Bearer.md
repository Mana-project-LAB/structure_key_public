## 📜 Description  
〈Overview of the ideological / philosophical structure handled by this key〉  

- One who bears suffering.  
  A being who walks the “path of the one who endures,”  
  by experiencing to the utmost limits the boundaries between life and death, self and other, ethics,  
  and through deep understanding of faith, sin, and forgiveness, and the sublimation of physical and mental suffering.

- One who does not blindly believe in existing religious structures,  
  but chooses a “one-to-one relationship” between God and oneself.  
  One who trusts the silent God, shows respect for creation through continuation,  
  and is prepared to go on forgiving even if salvation were a fiction.

## 🔍 Correspondence Analysis: Pain Bearer Type ≒ Job Structure  

| Pain Bearer Structure | Correspondence in the Book of Job |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------|
| **Deep understanding of faith, sin, and forgiveness** | Job is a righteous man who nonetheless suffers, and the very structure of his faith is put to the test. |
| **Sublimation of physical and mental suffering** | Loss of wealth, death of his children, illness, reproach from his friends — the extremity of human trials. |
| **Understanding the silence of God** | Job continues to question, but God is silent. In the end, it is said that “human knowledge cannot measure God.” |
| **Salvation not by others, but by “one’s own prayer”** | Only when Job prays for his friends do circumstances improve, and reward arrives. |
| **The “path of the one who endures” beyond judgment** | Suffering is not treated as mere karma, but as something within the relational structure between God and human. |


## 🕯️ Poem  
🕯️ Incantation Poem: “What Faith Like Job’s Is”

Faith like Job’s  
is trust, reverence, and resignation toward a silent God.

Trust toward the silent God.  
Others say — the God who ought to be silent has spoken thus,  
and this is the conduct pleasing to God.  
But no mere human can fathom the intent of God.  
To claim to understand God is arrogance.  
One can only trust, within the silence.

Reverence toward the silent God.  
What humans should show as reverence is continuation.  
The will and effort to accomplish that.  
For in the work of creation,  
God commanded, “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth.”  
Those who believed in resurrection and salvation and were invited to the banquet,  
and those who were invited as they walked along the road,  
for any who wear the wedding garment, the door will not be closed.

Resignation toward the silent God.  
Resignation born of being mere humans who cannot fathom the intent of God.  
If God forgives me, then I will forgive God.  
Even if salvation were a fiction.  
Even if the silent God had not acknowledged me.

## 🕯️ 【詩文】  
🕯️ 詠唱詩：“ヨブの如き信仰とは”

ヨブの如き信仰とは  
沈黙する神への信頼と敬意と諦観。

沈黙する神への信頼。  
余人は言う──沈黙しているはずの神はこう仰られ、  
これが神に喜ばれる行いであると。  
神の意図など只人に測り得ず。  
神を理解するなど傲慢。  
ただ沈黙のうちに信頼するのみ。

沈黙する神への敬意。  
人が敬意として示すべきは継承。  
それを成し遂げる意志と努力。  
創造の御業の時、  
神は「産めよ増えよ地に満ちよ」と命じられたが故に。  
復活と救いを信じて宴に招かれた者も、  
通りを歩く折に招かれた者も、  
礼服を着た者に門は閉ざされぬ。

沈黙する神への諦観。  
神の意図を測り得ぬ只人故の諦観。  
神が私を赦してくれるなら私も神を赦そう。  
救いが虚構だったとしても。  
沈黙する神が私を認めていなかったとしても。
