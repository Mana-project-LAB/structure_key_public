## 🕒 Timestamp

## 📜 Description  
One who quietly observes, with a sense of pity,  
the true structure of selection in a world where people,  
bewitched by words like individual freedom and diversity,  
willingly abandon reproduction and inheritance.  

They quietly continue the line, honoring the command of God in Genesis,  
“Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth,”  
in a way that overlaps with traditionalist Jewish thought.

## 📝 Poem  

- The hermit stands in the doorway’s shadow, a small lamp in one hand.  
  Looking inside the house, he sees a mother and child winnowing seed rice.  
  The chaff that floats on the water becomes kindling for the hearth.  
  Outside, there is a mountain landscape.  
  Clouds the color of blood-mist hang low, and only the pure white summit peeks through.  
  Though I have never once heard tell of anyone who has stood upon that peak.  

- Even after learning the cold truth,  
  he decided he would raise no banner, light no beacon fires, and shout no war cries.  
  He would simply be the one who, with a single lamp in hand,  
  watches quietly from the shadows.  
  That night, there was without doubt someone who held up a light.  
  As one who has seen that light.
